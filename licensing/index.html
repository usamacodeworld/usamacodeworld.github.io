Example journey

Getting licensed in Saudi Arabia

The typical SCFHS pathway for an internationally trained healthcare professional.

🇸🇦

Saudi Arabia

Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS)

9

Steps

~15

Weeks typical

Every step is tracked in your MCI Career dashboard with required documents, deadlines and your case manager's notes.

  1. 1

    Profile Creation

    ~3 days · day 3

    Create your MCI Career profile and Mumaris Plus account with personal, education and experience details.

    Passport copy Personal photo (white background)
  2. 2

    Document Preparation

    ~10 days · day 13

    Collect, attest and scan all credentials in the formats required by SCFHS and DataFlow.

    Degree certificate Academic transcript Internship certificate Experience certificates Current license CV
  3. 3

    DataFlow Verification

    ~25 days · day 38

    Primary Source Verification of your degree, license and experience by DataFlow Group on behalf of SCFHS.

    DataFlow application form Letter of authorization Documents from previous step
  4. 4

    Professional Classification

    ~15 days · day 53

    SCFHS assesses your qualifications and experience to determine your professional title (e.g. Consultant, Specialist, Resident).

    DataFlow report Board certificate (if applicable)
  5. 5

    Prometric / Licensing Exam

    ~20 days · day 73

    Book and pass the SCFHS licensing exam at a Prometric centre (exemptions apply for some board certifications).

    Exam eligibility number Prometric booking confirmation
  6. 6

    SCFHS Registration

    ~7 days · day 80

    Complete professional registration on Mumaris Plus and pay registration fees.

    Exam result Classification certificate Registration fee receipt
  7. 7

    Employer Requirements

    ~10 days · day 90

    Employer-specific onboarding: signed offer, medical fitness, BLS/ACLS, privileging documents.

    Signed offer letter Medical fitness certificate BLS / ACLS card
  8. 8

    Final License

    ~5 days · day 95

    SCFHS issues your professional license linked to your employer.

  9. Ready to Work

    ~10 days · day 105

    Visa, Iqama and hospital onboarding are complete. Welcome to your new role.

    Visa copy Iqama
