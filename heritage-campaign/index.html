Why patients choose wide-awake surgery

Dr. Clarkson has performed office-based WALANT surgery since 2016, and pioneered Wide Awake VR, a virtual reality experience patients can wear during the procedure.

No general anesthesia

Local numbing only, so there's no grogginess and none of the risks that come with being put to sleep.

Less to prepare

No fasting, and pre-op tests, EKGs and clearance usually aren't needed.

Back to your day

Many patients use the hand for light tasks the same day and drive themselves home.

Often lower cost

No separate anesthesia bill or hospital facility fee, which helps with high deductibles.

Conditions we treat in the office

Many common hand problems can be fixed in a short office visit. If yours needs the operating room, we'll talk you through that too.

Carpal tunnel

Numbness, tingling or weakness in the hand, often worse at night.

Trigger finger

A finger or thumb that catches, locks or snaps when you bend it.

Ganglion and mucous cysts

Lumps on the wrist or finger joints that ache or get in the way.

Dupuytren's contracture

Fingers slowly pulling into the palm so the hand won't lie flat.

Cubital and nerve tunnels

Pins and needles in the ring and little fingers, or forearm nerve pain.

Tendon and nail injuries

Mallet finger, tendon repairs and nail bed repairs after an injury.

Common questions

Does wide-awake surgery hurt?

The area is fully numbed with local anesthetic before anything starts. Most patients feel some pressure or movement, but not pain.

Do I need someone to drive me?

Usually not. Because you aren't put to sleep, no accompanying adult is required and many patients drive themselves home.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most insurance plans. Our team will check your coverage when we call you back. If you have a high deductible or no coverage, ask about our self-pay pricing.

Is every hand problem treated in the office?

No. Joint replacements, some fractures and larger wrist surgeries are done in the operating room. Dr. Clarkson will tell you which setting is right for you.
