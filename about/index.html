About MCI Career

Healthcare Talent for a Brighter Tomorrow

MCI Career is the recruitment, licensing and career platform of MedConsult International (MCI) — built in Riyadh to connect healthcare organisations with the professionals who keep care running.

Talk to our teamOur partners

Our mission

Put the right clinician in the right role — faster, safely and fairly.

Health systems across the GCC are expanding faster than local talent pipelines can grow. At the same time, skilled nurses, physicians and allied health professionals around the world face long, opaque processes to practise abroad.

MCI Career closes that gap. We combine a specialised healthcare talent database, AI-assisted matching and hands-on licensing case management so employers receive practice-ready professionals and candidates arrive supported.

Mission

Connect healthcare organisations with qualified, licensed talent through a transparent, technology-enabled process.

Vision

Become the most trusted healthcare workforce partner in the region — People Powering Better Care.

Part of MedConsult International

MedConsult International provides healthcare consulting, workforce solutions and operational support to hospitals and clinics. MCI Career brings its recruitment and licensing expertise online.

What we do

Four services, one accountable partner

Healthcare recruitment

Single hires to full department openings across medicine, nursing, pharmacy, therapy, lab and administration.

Licensing & credentialing

Case-managed journeys through DataFlow verification, classification, exams and registration.

CV & ATS services

CV analysis and guidance so professionals present their experience the way hiring systems read it.

International mobility

Relocation, visa coordination and onboarding support for professionals moving across borders.

How we work

Patient safety first

Every placement starts with verified credentials. We never shortcut primary source verification or licensing requirements.

Ethical recruitment

Transparent terms for candidates, no hidden fees for professionals and fair, documented processes for employers.

Partnership

We act as an extension of hospital HR teams — accountable for outcomes, not just CVs sent.

Practical innovation

We use AI where it genuinely saves time — matching, CV analysis, document checks — and keep humans in every decision.

Our team

Recruiters, licensing specialists and clinicians

Our Riyadh-based team includes healthcare recruiters, licensing case managers with hands-on experience of SCFHS, DHA and DOH processes, and clinical advisors who review profiles before they reach employers.

  • Dedicated account managers for hospital partners
  • Licensing case managers assigned to every journey
  • Clinical reviewers for specialist and consultant roles
  • Multilingual support in English and Arabic
