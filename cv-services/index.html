CV & ATS Services

Make sure hiring systems read your CV the way you intend

Most hospitals screen applications with an applicant tracking system before a person sees them. Our ATS analysis scores your CV across ten healthcare-specific sections and tells you exactly what to fix.

Upload your CVSee a sample report

What we score

Ten sections, one clear score

Each section is scored 0–100 and weighted into your overall ATS readiness.

01

Structure

Clear, conventional section order that parsers recognise — summary, experience, education, licenses, skills.

02

Contact information

Name, phone with country code, professional email, current location and nationality in plain text.

03

Professional summary

Three to four lines stating your title, years of experience, specialty and the role you are targeting.

04

Experience

Reverse-chronological roles with hospital, department, bed count or unit size and measurable responsibilities.

05

Education

Degrees with institution, country and year; board certifications and postgraduate training listed separately.

06

Skills

Clinical competencies, procedures and systems (e.g. Cerner, Epic) that match the job description.

07

Keywords

Specialty terms and requirements from the target job — the words recruiters and ATS filters search for.

08

Formatting

Parser-friendly layout: no tables for content, no text in images or headers, standard fonts, PDF or DOCX.

09

Licensing information

License type, authority, number, status and expiry — plus BLS/ACLS and other mandatory certifications.

10

Healthcare terminology

Correct clinical vocabulary and abbreviations for your field, used consistently and in context.
